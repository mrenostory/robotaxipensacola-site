01 · The car

About Robotaxi Pensacola

Cybercab is a purpose-built autonomous robotaxi: two seats, no driver controls, hailed in the Robotaxi app. You request the ride and ride.

You don't steer. There's no wheel to grab and no pedal to miss. It's built to operate without a driver in the cabin.

  • 2seats
  • 0steering wheels
  • 0pedals

02 · How you ride

Using the App

You don't hail it from our site. Open the Robotaxi app, set pickup and drop-off, confirm the ride, and watch the vehicle on the map.

  1. 1Order

    Open the Robotaxi app, set your pickup and drop-off, and confirm the ride.

  2. 2Enter

    Watch the vehicle on the map, then get in when it pulls up at your pickup.

  3. 3Relax

    Sit back while the car follows the route to your drop-off. No one is at the controls.

Two seats. Cybercab is a two-seat car, so plan for one other person, not a family load.

Check the map. The app shows where service is actually available. If Pensacola isn't on that map yet, you can't ride here yet.

03 · Under the skin

How autonomy works

Cybercab is built as a robotaxi, not a regular car with the driver removed.

There's no steering wheel and there are no pedals. Cameras and software are meant to see the road, follow the route and complete the trip without a person at the controls.

  • No steering wheel
  • No pedals
  • Cameras + software
